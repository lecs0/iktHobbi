What is IMSA?

IMSA stands for the International Motor Sports Association, the organization behind several North American sports car racing series. Its premier competition is the IMSA WeatherTech SportsCar Championship, where teams and manufacturers race at circuits across the United States and Canada.

The championship is known for multiclass racing: different types of cars share the track at the same time, but compete for wins and championship points within their own class. Faster prototype cars must navigate their way through traffic, while GT drivers battle other cars in their category.

The 2026 field includes GTP and LMP2 prototypes, as well as GTD PRO and GTD cars based on production sports cars. GTD PRO features professional driver lineups, while GTD includes a mix of professional and amateur drivers. Not every class races at every event.

Races range from shorter sprints to long endurance events, including the Rolex 24 At Daytona and the Twelve Hours of Sebring. Drivers share a car and take turns behind the wheel, so pit stops, reliability, traffic and team strategy can be just as important as outright speed.
